CBG Pro is a milestone for backgammon on Android: the complete GNU Backgammon engine -- the same code, the same neural nets, the same numbers as the desktop reference -- under a modern touch interface, with an analysis, training, and record-keeping suite that has no counterpart on the platform. Entirely offline: no server, no account, no ads, no telemetry -- the app does not request the network permission at all. Free software, GPL-3.0.

Why there is nothing similar:

* The engine is not "based on" GNU Backgammon -- it IS GNU Backgammon, vendored verbatim with byte-identical neural-net weights. Every number on screen is the engine's own; the app never substitutes its own backgammon logic.
* Regulation rollouts on a phone: 1296-trial, cubeful, variance-reduced, quasi-random -- the discipline desktop analysts use -- seed-reproducible, with the desktop-comparison harness published in the source repository.
* A Coach that is correct or silent: every explanation is licensed by the engine's numbers; when the numbers don't justify a sentence, it says less rather than inventing a reason.
* Your career, signed: finished matches enter a tamper-evident, cryptographically chained record -- your results, provable, in plain files you own.
* Your Personal Stats: gnubg's own match analysis -- error rates, luck, cube handling -- every number labeled with what it is.
* A tournament match clock, offered upstream to the GNU Backgammon project.
* Reproducible builds, with the verification script in the repository.

Play

Full matches at any length, seven strength levels from Beginner to Grandmaster. The complete doubling cube, Crawford, Jacoby, automatic doubles, beavers, and the canonical match-equity tables. A live tutor and the match clock when you want them.

Train

The Coach judges every move: best, fine-but-not-best (with what beat it), or flagged (with the equity it cost). Tap any better alternative and watch it play out on the board against your own. Undo, try the better idea, feel the difference. In longer matches it judges your cube actions too.

Analyse

Paste a GNU BG ID, Match ID or XGID -- or build any position on the set-up editor board. With dice, the engine ranks the plays; without dice, the cube decision. When ranking isn't enough: roll the position out, right on the device.

Review

Save matches as standard SGF and step through them move by move: what you played, what the engine preferred, the cost, the verdict. Files open unchanged in desktop GNU Backgammon and Backgammon Studio.

Your matches, your career, and your settings live in a folder you choose -- plain files, readable by desktop gnubg today and by anything else in twenty years. Nothing is sent anywhere; there is no "anywhere" in this app.

Requires Android 12 or newer and a 64-bit ARM device.
