# CBG Plus — The Advanced Guide (CBG Plus)

*The ultimate offline companion to everything you already play, analyse,
and browse.*

You have XG on the desktop, or you will. You play on Galaxy, or Heroes, or
at the club with a clock ticking beside the board. You read BGO. Nothing
here asks you to give any of that up — CBG Plus is built for the other
half of your backgammon life: the plane, the train, the hotel bar, the
tournament hall between rounds, the sofa when the Wi-Fi dies. It is a
complete, world-class backgammon study environment that owes **nothing to
any server, account, or subscription** — and it does three things no app
in the field does at all.

## The engine is the real thing

CBG runs the genuine GNU Backgammon engine — the same neural nets, the
same evaluations, the same numbers the desktop prints — natively on your
phone, at full strength. Every equity is gnubg's equity. Every error is
graded on gnubg's scale — doubtful, bad, very bad — in EMG, the way you
already read mistakes. When CBG shows you a number, you can sit down at
desktop gnubg and get the same number. That sentence is the whole design
philosophy, and it comes up again below in a way that will make you smile.

## The table: match play, done properly

Full matches against the engine at seven strengths, from casual to its
complete playing strength. Match equity handled correctly through Crawford
and post-Crawford using a proper MET. The cube works the way your hands
expect: **tap the doubling cube to double** — no menus. Dice are instant —
no wobbling animation to sit through — and a tap on the dice swaps the
play order. And there is a **competition match clock**: the tournament
regulation, not a settings farm — 12-second delay plus a bank of two
minutes per point, one clock shown, in the colour of the chequers of the
player on roll. Run out of time and you lose the match, exactly as you
would at the table. If you play live events, your practice can finally
feel like your Saturdays.

## Your career — the record no one else can offer

This is the first thing with no competitor. Every tournament match you
finish is silently analysed by the engine and recorded into **your
career**: the match file, the full analysis, and a cryptographically
signed, chained record of all of it. Not "saved to our cloud." Signed, on
your device, into files you own — and here is the part for the sceptics
among you (we know you, you are our people): the record can be verified by
**a small public script using nothing but openssl**, on any computer, by
anyone, without trusting us. If a single die of a single game had been
altered after the fact, the chain says so, and says where.

Your results, your error rates, your progress across months — provable.
Screenshots can be doctored. Cloud databases can be edited. A signed chain
cannot, and yours lives in your pocket. When you tell the club your error
rate dropped two points this season, you can *show* them, in a way that
would satisfy a cryptographer.

## The coach that never rambles

You have seen engine "explanations" that talk confidently past the
position. CBG's coach mode is built on one law: **correct or silent**.
When it speaks, it says something gnubg's numbers actually support; when
the position is beyond honest words, it shows you the numbers and holds
its tongue. And the coaching loop is the grandmaster's method, not the
tutor's: before you commit a play, you can walk through the candidate
plays **on the board, graphically**, each with its equity and a curated
reason — survey the whole field first, then decide. Practice matches in
coach mode are the gym: they sharpen you, and they never touch your
career record. What goes on your record, you played alone.

## The analyst's bench — rollouts in your pocket

Paste any position as a GNU Backgammon ID, or build one in the editor.
You get gnubg's candidate plays with equities, full cube analysis —
double/no-double, take/pass, with the action equities laid out — and then
the second thing nobody else offers: **real rollouts, on the phone**.
Cubeful, variance-reduced, quasi-random dice, 1296 trials per candidate —
the regulation rollout, running on your device's own cores, live, with no
cloud on the other end. Switch apps, pocket the phone: it keeps rolling
and tells you when it is done.

And because the engine is the real thing, every rollout prints its
**seed**. Take that seed to desktop gnubg, set the same trials and
settings, and roll the same position: same dice, same numbers. We do not
ask you to trust our rollouts — we hand you the reproduction recipe. Find
another app that dares to do that.

## What it will never do

No account. No cloud. No subscription. No telemetry, no analytics, no
"phone home" — the app has no network code to phone home *with*. Nothing
expires, nothing is rented, nothing waits on a server being up or a
company staying solvent. Built on the GPL — the engine that has served
this game for twenty-five years, in the open, where you can check it.

Airplane mode is not a limitation. It is home turf.

## Who this is for

The player who studies. The player who competes live and wants practice
under the clock. The player who keeps score of their own game honestly
and wants a record that can survive an argument. The player who owns XG
and loves it and still spends half their backgammon hours somewhere XG is
not. There is no fight to pick here — everything else you use does what
it does. This is the companion piece: complete when you are connected,
and just as complete when you are not — with a signed career, on-device
rollouts, and a coach under oath, none of which exist anywhere else.

*CBG Plus. The whole game, in your pocket, owing nothing to anyone.*
